# Code of conduct

This project is a small scientific and engineering effort. We want it to be a
place where people can ask questions, report problems and disagree about
technical points without fear of being belittled.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticise code, results and ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback graciously, and credit other people's work.
- Keep discussion on topic: the software, its physics and its documentation.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated unwelcome contact.

## Scope

This applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who
break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer, GitHub user
[Francesco-Cini](https://github.com/Francesco-Cini), privately. Reports are
treated confidentially.

This text is adapted in spirit from the
